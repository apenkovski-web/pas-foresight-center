About the Center

Connecting regional insight with the bigger picture.

A space for careful analysis of change across Central and Eastern Europe, Europe and the transatlantic community.

Political decisions, economic shifts, technological change and social developments rarely happen in isolation. They interact across borders and time horizons, creating new risks as well as new possibilities.

PAS Foresight Center brings these developments into view. Its focus is on understanding how change is unfolding, why it matters for the region, and what questions institutions and communities may need to consider as they plan for the future.

Our guiding idea

“The future is not a prediction to receive. It is a set of possibilities to examine.”

How we look at change

Three connected perspectives.

A regional issue can have national roots, European consequences and transatlantic implications. We consider those levels together.

01 — Place

From local to regional

We examine how national developments connect to shared regional questions and different experiences across Central and Eastern Europe.

02 — Connection

Across institutions and borders

We follow the relationships among states, institutions, societies and partners that shape collective responses to change.

03 — Time

From immediate pressures to long horizons

We place current developments in context and consider the slower trends and possible turning points that may influence what follows.

Our approach

Make uncertainty easier to examine.

Foresight does not predict one outcome. It helps people ask better questions and prepare for more than one possibility.

Scan

Notice emerging signals, new developments and early signs of change across relevant fields.

Connect

Interpret signals alongside established trends, regional context and the relationships between issues.

Explore

Consider different plausible futures and examine how assumptions or events could shift the picture.

Prepare

Draw out implications, questions and choices that can help institutions think and act with greater awareness.
