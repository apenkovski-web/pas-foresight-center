PAS Foresight Center examines the political, economic, security and societal forces shaping Central and Eastern Europe and its relationships with the wider world.

Contact the Center

Areas of focus

Regional insight, connected to global change

Our work brings regional knowledge together with a forward-looking view of the issues that influence policy, institutions and communities.

Central and Eastern Europe

We follow the region’s political and economic development, shifting strategic priorities, and the forces changing its place in Europe. Our perspective considers the diversity of national experiences as well as the regional trends linking them.

Transatlantic relations

We examine relations between Europe and North America, including changing policy priorities, alliance cooperation, shared democratic commitments and the ways domestic developments on both sides of the Atlantic shape their common agenda.

Security and resilience

We explore how states and societies respond to strategic uncertainty, hybrid risks, information challenges and pressure on democratic institutions. The focus is on understanding long-term implications and options for strengthening resilience.

Europe’s future and cooperation

We consider the evolving European policy landscape, relations among institutions and member states, and the prospects for cooperation on issues that cross borders—from energy and technology to economic security and social change.

Geopolitical and economic trends

We track shifts in global power, trade, investment and technology that may affect Europe’s choices. Foresight helps identify emerging questions early and assess how different developments could interact.

Strategic foresight

We use horizon scanning, trend analysis and scenario thinking to make uncertainty easier to examine. These methods help decision-makers test assumptions, consider alternative futures and prepare for a range of outcomes.

Our approach

Evidence, context and a long view

We connect current events with deeper structural trends. Our approach is interdisciplinary: political, economic, security and societal developments are considered together, with attention to how they differ across countries and communities.

Foresight does not claim to predict a single future. It offers a disciplined way to explore what may change, what could remain uncertain, and which choices can help organizations prepare. The Center aims to make complex developments clear and useful for public discussion and strategic decision-making.
